## Accepted behavior

- Full listing principal is required: a 500 USDT listing needs 500 USDT. Commission short-payment tolerance never reduces principal funding or delivery.
- Owner approval remains separate from funding. A deposit cannot bypass listing review.
- Partial reservations conserve inventory, with at most three active trades for a seller. The amount-correction coordinator's one-active-trade cohort and the initial coordinator's one selected mapping are separate development restrictions; neither changes that general policy.
- The buyer receives the full principal. The 1% buyer fee in fiat and the seller's combined 2% USDT commission remain separate.
- A buyer's paid claim is not release authority. Trusted independent payment evidence and seller authorization are both required.
- A payout timeout keeps funds reserved and preserves the original provider identity. A missing lookup does not permit a replacement send.
- The isolated V4 prepayment cancellation returns the exact original reservation to its original listing once. It creates no payout or commission. Its supplied simulation assertions do not authorize cancellation in either existing canonical cohort.
- Standalone wallet restoration starts paused because external transfers may have continued after its checkpoint. The v2 amount-correction and v3 initial cohorts each have a distinct combined format requiring a paused source and a paused credential-free target. Their old standalone formats continue failing closed after canonical activation. Restored history is verified offline; it supplies no authority re-entry, source leadership or resume.

These rules are executable in the isolated development model. Two separate private coordinators couple actual canonical SQL and stored authentication to one existing amount-correction proposal or one ordinary initial seller acceptance. Their development scopes do not connect production marketplace behavior, remaining lifecycle transitions or public web/mobile interfaces. Those integrations must preserve consent, eligibility, reviews and completion rules.

## Current private amount-correction cohort

An owner can explicitly enroll, inspect and pause one already accepted canonical request with a clean existing wallet reservation and a valid pending `amount_correction` proposal. This correction cohort supports current ILS fee policies, Bank Transfer and Face-to-Face. It requires the only active canonical reservation on its listing, the only nonterminal wallet trade globally and no unfinished payout.

Buyer acceptance resolves the actual web or mobile session/account rows through the corresponding fixed reader inside the same `SERIALIZABLE` transaction as canonical reads, wallet replay and writes. The mobile reader also binds the token to its stored device/platform and enforces the current minimum app version. Fresh ownership/eligibility checks precede historical receipt lookup, and authority/expiry are rechecked after persistence. Both transports share the unchanged acceptance identity: a current valid session can recover the original receipt, including after pause, while revoked credentials are denied. Canonical terms, the principal adjustment, immutable receipt, deterministic system message/notification/audit and safe publication intent commit together. SQL guards fence unsupported enrolled-listing writers and standalone wallet access after activation.

This cohort has no public web/mobile route, account/trade screen integration, notification/realtime delivery, provider or signer. It cannot create a new proposal, perform initial trade acceptance, cancel, handle payment/dispute/release/settlement transitions or resume after pause. Owner `inspect` and `pause` remain supported through either private credential entry point. A current web or mobile owner can export the complete supported recovery unit after pausing the source. Fresh-target restore and offline same-target inspection preserve the financial records, add a new pause and exclude usable credentials; neither mobile authentication table is restored. Existing wallet-only formats still cannot recover an activated cohort; account-authority re-entry and resume remain unimplemented. The [coordinator design](marketplace-coordinator-design.md), [mobile authority contract](marketplace-mobile-auth-design.md), [combined recovery contract](marketplace-recovery-design.md) and [recovery runbook](recovery-runbook.md) define these boundaries.

## Current V4 cancellation and canonical groundwork

The isolated V4 ledger has a closed `continueFromV3` / `cancelInitialTrade` / `pause` command set. It preserves the frozen original histories, exact acceptance and party/asset/destination bindings, and the inherited pause. One immutable cancellation receipt records the full-principal return to the same listing. The native fixture returns 200 USDT once, leaving 500 in listing inventory and zero reserved for that trade, with no payout, fee or provider submission. Original acceptance and cancellation receipts remain recoverable, while inherited unknown payouts retain their original identities and cannot dispatch again.

The distinct `isolated-v4-ledger` format-4 backup retains all four engine histories and all three continuations. It requires a paused source, restores atomically into a fresh target with a new V4 quarantine pause, and supports same-target inspection after an unknown COMMIT without overwriting a committed target. It refuses both existing coordinated namespaces. It grants no stored participant authority, source leadership or resume.

The separate canonical planner/participant supports accepted initial-style Bank Transfer/Face-to-Face trades with unchanged listing-price terms, the current fee policy and one of the two supported simulated assets. It verifies exact request/trade/listing dependencies, actor-specific bank disclosure and retained payment/evidence/disputes, then closes the proposal, archives reminders, reopens the listing and records audit/notices and trust intent. It refuses Wise, Cardless, demand matches, adjusted terms, expired listings, competing reservations and inconsistent aliases. The native mechanics drill uses a synthetic operator pause and **zero financial returns**. Its committed cancellation is not an authenticated V4 cancellation or cross-system financial receipt. The frozen initial/correction coordinators, their writer guards and their combined recovery formats remain unchanged and reject cancellation. [Current source-bound verification](prepayment-cancellation-verification-2026-11-06.md) covers these two separate foundations.

The current ledger rebuilds all historical commands and serializes the whole wallet. That is useful correctness evidence, but it is not a production throughput or availability design. The pure chain decoder validates supplied fixture consistency; it does not authenticate consensus or a blockchain node. The local role switcher is a rehearsal tool; it is not account login. These distinctions must remain visible in reviews and release notes.

## Next decisions and implementation sequence

1. **Implement the authenticated cancellation successor for the existing mapped initial trade.** Introduce an explicit coordinated profile/namespace, predecessor checkpoint and reviewed migration while preserving the old engines, cohorts and fixtures. Derive cancellation-specific seller/buyer authority from current stored web/mobile sessions, accounts, device bindings and revocation state; new-sale `canSell`/`canBuy` alone is not cancellation authority. One certified SERIALIZABLE transaction must commit canonical cancellation, the V4 full-principal return, original immutable cross-system receipt and safe publication intent, with authority rechecked after awaited persistence. SQL fences must order payment claims, stored/embedded evidence, bank disclosure, disputes, expiry, revocation and competing/direct writers. A current valid participant must recover the original receipt after retry or pause without another return; a manually cancelled canonical row is insufficient. The owner force-cancel override remains outside automatic custody release.
2. **Complete that successor's combined recovery before calling the integration finished.** Preserve all v1/v2/v3/v4 histories, three continuations, original activation/acceptance/cancellation receipts and complete canonical records/events/publication. Require current-owner web/mobile export from a paused source, atomic fresh-target restoration with a new pause and credential quarantine, and same-target inspection after unknown COMMIT. Retain the existing initial/correction formats unchanged. Exact-source retained tests and native authority/writer races, rollback, cross-transport receipt and combined recovery drills are required before adding a public product route. These simulation tasks can proceed without a connected signer.
3. **Develop the Trust Wallet receiving and signing integration against its reviewed capability boundaries.** The receiving-interface preference is resolved; [custody-decision.md](custody-decision.md) records the remaining recovery and chain-connected signer/testnet environment requirements. Public-address observation does not require a wallet seed. Exact chain/asset/address configuration, account eligibility, key control, operational limits and recovery ownership still need concrete evidence; no personal seed belongs in the application server. The Trust Wallet preference does not change BSC asset provenance or establish unattended payout guarantees.
4. **Define the fiat verification and later lifecycle contracts.** Bank and cash payments need a concrete trusted evidence authority and exception policy. Neither the buyer's paid button nor a manual completion flag supplies that evidence. Implement buyer claims/dispute freezes, subsequent proposals and full release/settlement through explicit financial and canonical transitions that preserve old history. Before admitting competing commitments, implement complete bidirectional canonical/wallet identity coverage and migration/recovery compatibility; matching totals cannot prove buyer, destination, quote or request identity. Preserve the general three-trade policy, eligibility/reviews, ranks, benefits and separate fees; connect reviewed deduplicated delivery and actual web/mobile interfaces.
5. **Extend production recovery authority beyond the isolated formats.** Define account-authority re-entry, source leadership/fencing, authenticated encrypted retention, production roles and external reconciliation. Current guards enforce their application protocol, not hostile superuser DDL after every deferred event has been deliberately flushed. A successful isolated restore never authorizes an active second copy or resume.
6. **Complete actual provider/testnet drills.** In the separately configured eligible test environment, demonstrate deposit attribution, wrong-token rejection, finality, duplicate observations, gas resources, payout policy, uncertain submission, worker restart, stale backup and signer recovery. Preserve immutable payout identity and require evidence that recovery cannot submit twice.
7. **Finish operations and independent review.** Review real permissions and signing policy, recovery and limits, monitoring and incident ownership, user-facing acceptance and deployment plan. Record unresolved findings explicitly; this development verification is not an independent security audit.
8. **Present the exact release for launch authorization.** No automatic threshold, green test result or broad development approval authorizes mainnet funding, signing, production migration, deployment or real transfers.
