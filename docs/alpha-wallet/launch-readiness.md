## Accepted behavior

- Full listing principal is required: a 500 USDT listing needs 500 USDT. Commission short-payment tolerance never reduces principal funding or delivery.
- Owner approval remains separate from funding. A deposit cannot bypass listing review.
- Partial reservations conserve inventory, with at most three active trades for a seller. The amount-correction coordinator's one-active-trade cohort and the initial coordinator's one selected mapping are separate development restrictions; neither changes that general policy.
- The buyer receives the full principal. The 1% buyer fee in fiat and the seller's combined 2% USDT commission remain separate.
- A buyer's paid claim is unverified and preserves the full principal reservation. V5 records the exact claim and permits only that buyer to freeze a dispute after it, with no account or journal movement. Trusted independent payment verification and seller authorization are both required before any future release.
- A payout timeout keeps funds reserved and preserves the original provider identity. A missing lookup does not permit a replacement send.
- The isolated V4 financial command returns the full original reservation once. The separate authenticated cancellation successor derives its evidence from current stored authority and locked canonical rows, then commits the return, canonical effects and immutable receipt/publication together. It creates no payout or commission.
- Explicit enrollment into the V5 payment profile disables new cancellation, including for an unpaid accepted trade. Previously cancelled V4 mappings remain archival. Existing V4 profiles retain their cancellation behavior until enrollment; a complete unpaid cancellation path requires an explicit later profile before product integration.
- Standalone wallet restoration starts paused because external transfers may have continued after its checkpoint. Correction, initial, cancellation and payment cohorts each have a distinct combined format requiring a paused source and a paused credential-free target. The payment format preserves V1–V5 history, both predecessors and exact private evidence bytes. Older formats retain their scopes. Restored history supplies no authority re-entry, source leadership or resume.

These rules are implemented in the isolated development model, with current payment verification status recorded above. Private coordinators couple canonical SQL and stored authority to correction, initial acceptance, cancellation, and payment-claim/post-claim-dispute profiles. Existing production product behavior is unchanged. Trusted payment verification, seller release, dispute resolution and full unpaid cancellation remain future lifecycle work; product integration must preserve consent, eligibility, reviews and completion rules.

## Current private amount-correction cohort

An owner can explicitly enroll, inspect and pause one already accepted canonical request with a clean existing wallet reservation and a valid pending `amount_correction` proposal. This correction cohort supports current ILS fee policies, Bank Transfer and Face-to-Face. It requires the only active canonical reservation on its listing, the only nonterminal wallet trade globally and no unfinished payout.

Buyer acceptance resolves the actual web or mobile session/account rows through the corresponding fixed reader inside the same `SERIALIZABLE` transaction as canonical reads, wallet replay and writes. The mobile reader also binds the token to its stored device/platform and enforces the current minimum app version. Fresh ownership/eligibility checks precede historical receipt lookup, and authority/expiry are rechecked after persistence. Both transports share the unchanged acceptance identity: a current valid session can recover the original receipt, including after pause, while revoked credentials are denied. Canonical terms, the principal adjustment, immutable receipt, deterministic system message/notification/audit and safe publication intent commit together. SQL guards fence unsupported enrolled-listing writers and standalone wallet access after activation.

This cohort has no public web/mobile route, account/trade screen integration, notification/realtime delivery, provider or signer. It cannot create a new proposal, perform initial trade acceptance, cancel, handle payment/dispute/release/settlement transitions or resume after pause. Owner `inspect` and `pause` remain supported through either private credential entry point. A current web or mobile owner can export the complete supported recovery unit after pausing the source. Fresh-target restore and offline same-target inspection preserve the financial records, add a new pause and exclude usable credentials; neither mobile authentication table is restored. Existing wallet-only formats still cannot recover an activated cohort; account-authority re-entry and resume remain unimplemented. The [coordinator design](marketplace-coordinator-design.md), [mobile authority contract](marketplace-mobile-auth-design.md), [combined recovery contract](marketplace-recovery-design.md) and [recovery runbook](recovery-runbook.md) define these boundaries.

It preserves the frozen original histories, exact acceptance and party/asset/destination bindings, and the inherited pause. One immutable cancellation receipt records the full-principal return to the same listing. The native fixture returns 200 USDT once, leaving 500 in listing inventory and zero reserved for that trade, with no payout, fee or provider submission. Original acceptance and cancellation receipts remain recoverable, while inherited unknown payouts retain their original identities and cannot dispatch again.

The distinct `isolated-v4-ledger` format-4 backup retains all four engine histories and all three continuations. It requires a paused source, restores atomically into a fresh target with a new V4 quarantine pause, and supports same-target inspection after an unknown COMMIT without overwriting a committed target. It refuses coordinated sources, including both original namespaces. It grants no stored participant authority, source leadership or resume.

The separate canonical planner/participant supports accepted initial-style Bank Transfer/Face-to-Face trades with unchanged listing-price terms, the current fee policy and one of the two supported simulated assets. It verifies exact request/trade/listing dependencies, actor-specific bank disclosure and retained payment/evidence/disputes, then closes the proposal, archives reminders, reopens the listing and records audit/notices and trust intent. It refuses Wise, Cardless, demand matches, adjusted terms, expired listings, competing reservations and inconsistent aliases. The native mechanics drill uses a synthetic operator pause and **zero financial returns**. Its committed cancellation is not an authenticated V4 cancellation or cross-system financial receipt. The frozen initial/correction coordinators, their writer guards and their combined recovery formats remain unchanged and reject cancellation. [Historical source-bound verification](prepayment-cancellation-verification-2026-11-06.md) covers these two separate foundations.

## Retained authenticated cancellation successor

The new `alpha_wallet_cancellation_marketplace_simulation` profile explicitly upgrades one original accepted initial mapping under current owner authority. It preserves original activation/acceptance/publication and V1–V3 history, appends the source-bound V4 continuation, switches only the reviewed SQL hooks and records its immutable transition atomically. An inherited pause stays effective. Preparation-only and continuation-without-transition attempts cannot commit.

Current stored web/mobile buyer or seller authority derives the complete cancellation from locked canonical rows and original accepted wallet evidence. New-sale `canBuy`/`canSell` is not cancellation authority; disabled accounts and invalid sessions are still refused, and an unrelated owner cannot cancel. A fresh cancellation requires an unpaused, unexpired supported ordinary Bank/F2F trade. Buyer bank-details disclosure blocks buyer cancellation; the seller may still cancel an otherwise supported unpaid trade. One cancellation transaction returns the full reserved principal to its original listing, persists the exact canonical effects, and retains one cross-system receipt and safe publication. Valid same-party retries recover that receipt across transports or after pause. Public outputs omit credentials and private bank/destination data.

SQL independently reconstructs the canonical effects and requires complete financial/canonical/receipt/publication coherence. The entire operation shares its earliest physical transaction certificate, including nested authentication and recovery readers. Legacy mapped-listing payment/evidence/dispute/settlement writers remain fenced until explicit successor participants exist. The original correction and initial APIs and formats remain separate.

The distinct `alpha-wallet-cancellation-marketplace-simulation-backup` envelope supports paused accepted and cancelled successor sources. Current owner web/mobile export preserves complete canonical rows, original and successor evidence and all four financial histories. Fresh-target restoration appends one quarantine pause, restores disabled credential-free identities and seals further commands, canonical changes, login re-entry and resume. Same-target inspection handles uncertain COMMIT without overwriting a claimed target. See the [design](cancellation-marketplace-coordinator-design.md), [verified evidence](cancellation-marketplace-verification-2026-11-06.md) and [runbook](recovery-runbook.md).

## Current payment-claim and dispute successor and complete recovery

The new payment profile explicitly enrolls one original V4 cancellation mapping under current owner authority. Enrollment requires both a version-upgrade acknowledgement and acknowledgement of the narrower payment-claim-only scope. It retains original V1–V4 history and coordination evidence, records the V5 continuation and switches the reviewed writer guards atomically. An inherited pause remains effective. **After enrollment, no new cancellation is available in V5.** A previously cancelled V4 source can enter only as an archival state and cannot regain reserved funds.

The actual current buyer can report payment on the exact accepted trade. Bank Transfer requires one atomic PNG, JPEG or PDF attachment, with canonical base64, signature validation, a maximum size of 8 MiB, deterministic neutral metadata and a SHA-256 content commitment. Face-to-Face permits no attachment. The claim changes the canonical request/trade to payment sent and the locked listing to in trade, while the wallet records payment reported. It preserves the full principal reservation, inventory, parties, terms, fees, disclosure and destination. The attachment remains an **unverified buyer assertion**; it grants no trusted-verification, seller-release or payout authority.

Only that current buyer can open a dispute, and only after this profile's exact claim. The private normalized reason and retained buyer evidence bind the new dispute to the original claim. The trade remains payment reported with a dispute freeze; accounts, journal, principal and payout state remain unchanged. Claim/dispute actions record deterministic timeline, notification and audit effects, archive the applicable action reminders and clear reminder/inactivity state. The dispute freeze does not change the existing product's reminder or trust behavior. Listing expiry alone does not prove nonpayment or release the reservation; this profile can record a claim after expiry when the accepted request and exact reservation still exist.

Current stored web/mobile authority, canonical reads and exact evidence bytes, V5 effects, immutable coordination receipts and safe publication share the same earliest physical transaction certificate. Matching buyer retries recover the original receipt across transports and after pause; changed proof, reason or actor cannot claim it. SQL independently validates complete canonical/financial effects and stored proof bytes. Public receipts/publications omit attachment bytes, private reason text, filenames and account data.

The separate payment combined backup retains complete original V1–V5 financial history, both predecessors, canonical rows and exact private attachment bytes. Current-owner export requires a paused source. A fresh-target restore appends one quarantine pause, preserves the original records, disables identities, excludes usable credentials/sessions and seals new commands or credential re-entry. Same-target inspection resolves uncertain restore outcomes without overwriting a committed target.

Existing web/mobile product behavior remains unchanged. No product route, screen, delivery worker, actual Trust Wallet connection or signer is installed. Trusted payment verification, seller-authorized release, dispute resolution and a complete unpaid cancellation path require explicit later profile work before product integration. See the [payment contract](payment-marketplace-coordinator-design.md), [current verification report](payment-marketplace-verification-2026-11-06.md) and verification status at the top of this document.

## Next decisions and implementation sequence

The [immutable admission, timed preparation and exact replay phase](deposit-observation-preparation.md) is implemented and verified in the current milestone. The immediate development slice is now the [remaining durable observation inbox](deposit-observation-ingestion-design.md): a separate store, persisted event/transaction/block anchors, conflict quarantine, finite candidate progress and independent paused recovery. The current callback is trusted synthetic context, not a database checkpoint provider. Schema, recorder, persistent cursor and backup remain unimplemented. Neither phase supplies ledger-credit authority or exhaustive chain coverage.

1. **Define trusted bank/cash payment verification.** Establish the independent evidence authority, exact amount/currency/recipient binding, audit trail and exception policy that can verify the retained buyer claim. A buyer's paid action, attachment, manual completion flag or owner assertion cannot substitute for that evidence or seller release authorization.
2. **Complete the lifecycle through an explicit later profile.** Add exact seller-authorized release, dispute resolution and the resulting settlement/refund transitions, together with a complete supported unpaid cancellation path. Current V5 deliberately has no new cancellation, even before a payment claim. Define disclosure, expiry, evidence and revocation ordering without inferring nonpayment or releasing reserved funds from elapsed time alone. Preserve all V1–V5 meanings, receipts, fixtures and recovery formats, capture independent V5 predecessor evidence, and use explicit continuation/migration for expanded authority. Each slice needs current web/mobile authority, one certified atomic transaction, independent SQL completeness, complete recovery and actual contention/rollback/uncertain-COMMIT evidence.
3. **Integrate the completed contracts into the actual product.** Connect website/mobile account, trade, evidence and receipt screens only after the private lifecycle covers all supported exits. Before admitting competing commitments, establish complete bidirectional canonical/wallet identity coverage and migration/recovery compatibility; matching totals cannot prove buyer, destination, quote or request identity. Preserve the general three-trade policy, eligibility/reviews, ranks, benefits and separate fees, and add reviewed deduplicated notification/realtime delivery. The existing synthetic English/Arabic rehearsal does not verify these product routes.
4. **Develop the Trust Wallet receiving and signing integration against its reviewed capability boundaries.** The receiving-interface preference is resolved; [custody-decision.md](custody-decision.md) records the remaining recovery and chain-connected signer/testnet environment requirements. Public-address observation does not require a wallet seed. Exact chain/asset/address configuration, account eligibility, key control, operational limits and recovery ownership still need concrete evidence; no personal seed belongs in the application server. The Trust Wallet preference does not change BSC asset provenance or establish unattended payout guarantees.
5. **Extend production recovery authority beyond the isolated formats.** Define account-authority re-entry, source leadership/fencing, authenticated encrypted retention, production roles and external reconciliation. Current guards enforce their application protocol, not hostile superuser DDL after every deferred event has been deliberately flushed. A successful isolated restore never authorizes an active second copy or resume.
6. **Complete actual provider/testnet drills.** In the separately configured eligible test environment, demonstrate deposit attribution, wrong-token rejection, finality, duplicate observations, gas resources, payout policy, uncertain submission, worker restart, stale backup and signer recovery. Preserve immutable payout identity and require evidence that recovery cannot submit twice.
7. **Finish operations and independent review.** Review real permissions and signing policy, recovery and limits, monitoring and incident ownership, user-facing acceptance and deployment plan. Record unresolved findings explicitly; this development verification is not an independent security audit.
8. **Present the exact release for launch authorization.** No automatic threshold, green test result or broad development approval authorizes mainnet funding, signing, production migration, deployment or real transfers.
